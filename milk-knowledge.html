MILK KNOWLEDGE

Different processes.
Different outcomes.

Compare the milk-processing methods recorded in the TEFA UHT interview.

PROCESS COMPARISON

Three methods
to understand.

Figures below follow the interview report. Confirm operating parameters with the facility before use.

Process Temperature Time Storage note in report
Pasteurization 70–80°C 15–20 seconds 7–10 days under refrigeration
Sterilization 121°C 5 minutes Refer to validated packaging and product instructions
UHT 135–150°C 2 seconds Up to 1 year unopened; 2–3 days refrigerated after opening, as stated in the interview
Educational summary based on the TEFA UHT interview document. It is not a substitute for validated process specifications, food safety procedures, or the storage instructions printed on a product label.
01 / PASTEURIZATION

Short-time heating

The interview describes pasteurization at 70–80°C for 15–20 seconds. It is intended to reduce pathogenic microorganisms while refrigerated storage remains necessary.

02 / STERILIZATION

Higher heat exposure

The interview describes sterilization at 121°C for 5 minutes. It notes a stronger heat treatment and possible changes in nutritional value and milk colour.

03 / UHT

Very high temperature, short time

The interview describes UHT processing at 135–150°C for around 2 seconds, followed by sterile packaging for extended unopened storage.

STORAGE MATTERS

Processing is only
part of the story.

Storage conditions and packaging play an important role after processing.

Pasteurized milk requires refrigeration. UHT products rely on an appropriate sterile packaging system for unopened shelf stability. Once opened, follow the product label and keep the product refrigerated as instructed.

For sterilized products, storage guidance should always follow the validated product and packaging specifications.
