ABOUT TEFA UHT

A learning environment shaped by industry.

TEFA UHT connects hands-on learning, liquid food processing, campus production, and product development.

OUR BACKGROUND

From TEFA Milk
to TEFA UHT.

Established around 2022–2023, the facility was initially known as TEFA Milk before adopting the TEFA UHT name.

Its scope expanded from milk to a wider range of liquid food products. Today, it operates as a mini-industry environment at Politeknik Negeri Jember.

It brings production practice and industry-oriented learning into one environment.

POLITEKNIK NEGERI JEMBER
THE THREE PILLARS

Three functions.
One ecosystem.

Three connected roles shape the TEFA UHT ecosystem.

01

Learning & Practicum

Students from different disciplines can learn about industrial processing stages, equipment operation, and troubleshooting. The facility has been used by students from animal science, food technology, and engineering-related areas, including machine and PLC systems.

02

Campus Production

TEFA UHT produces liquid food products to support internal campus needs, including canteen supply and events such as student orientation, graduation ceremonies, and campus anniversaries.

03

Industrial R&D

The facility serves as a place for pilot-scale product trials and industry collaboration. Every project requires an initial discussion to assess its material volume, process needs, and equipment readiness.
